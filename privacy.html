Ice Slide

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

Ice Slide does not collect, store or share any personal data about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

The short version. It's a game about a green cube jumping over ice. It doesn't want to know anything about you, and it doesn't ask.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent or share any personal information. Specifically, Ice Slide does not collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details
  • your location
  • your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, files or calendar
  • device identifiers, advertising IDs or any other identifier used for tracking
  • usage analytics, crash telemetry or behavioural data

The game requests no permissions on your device, because it needs none.

What is stored on your device

Ice Slide saves three values locally on your own device so the game remembers you between sessions:

  • your best score
  • which skin you have equipped
  • which free Shop modifiers you have switched on

These never leave your device and are never sent to us — we do not operate a server, so there is nowhere for them to go. Deleting the app removes them. Nothing in that list identifies you.

Leaderboards (optional)

If you choose to sign in, Ice Slide can post your best score to Google Play Games Services (Android) or Game Center (iOS) so it appears on a leaderboard.

  • This is entirely optional. Decline the sign-in prompt and the whole game still works exactly as before — nothing is locked behind it.
  • The only thing ever sent is a single number: your score.
  • That score is handled by Google or Apple under their privacy policies, not ours. We never receive it and we have no access to your Play Games or Game Center account.
  • Scores from runs with any Shop modifier switched on are not submitted at all.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy for how they handle that data.

Advertising and analytics

There are none. Ice Slide contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no third-party tracking libraries of any kind.

Purchases

There are none. The in-game Shop is free — it costs nothing, real or virtual, and grants no purchasable entitlement. There is no in-app purchase, no subscription and no currency.

Children

Ice Slide is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to delete, export or restrict.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data held about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to provide or erase. If you want to remove the values stored on your own device, delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If Ice Slide ever starts collecting data, this page will be updated before that change ships, and the store listing's privacy details will be updated to match. The date at the top shows when this page last changed.
